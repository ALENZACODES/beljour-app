# Third party notices

No third party source files are copied into this repository; the one third party asset in it is the Fraunces font file listed below. Everything below is installed as a dependency or loaded at runtime. `licences.json` is the full ledger, with what still needs a decision; `scripts/check_licences.py` fails the build on anything missing from it.

The CIEDE2000 colour difference is implemented from the published paper: G. Sharma, W. Wu, E. N. Dalal, "The CIEDE2000 color difference formula: implementation notes, supplementary test data, and mathematical observations", Color Research and Application, 2005. Its test pairs are used in `api/tests/test_shade.py`.

The sample product range in `api/plumb_api/data/shades.json` and the fibre shades in `api/plumb_api/services/fibre.py` are original and made up. They contain no brand names or real products.

The WCAG contrast check in `web/src/ui/contrast.ts` follows the WCAG 2.x definitions of relative luminance and contrast ratio. The thin plate spline in `api/plumb_api/services/tps.py` follows F. L. Bookstein, "Principal warps: thin-plate splines and the decomposition of deformations", IEEE PAMI, 1989.

The One Euro filter in `web/src/reel/oneEuro.ts` is written from the paper: G. Casiez, N. Roussel, D. Vogel, "1 Euro Filter: A Simple Speed-based Low-pass Filter for Noisy Input in Interactive Systems", CHI 2012. Body bands follow WHO adult BMI categories and NICE guideline NG246.
