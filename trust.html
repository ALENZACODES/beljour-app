Every line here is a switch or a fact about how this app works today. Anything that isn't built says so.

    My photos on this device

    A web app in your browser can't read your whole camera roll. Beljour only sees the photos you pick, one pick at a time.

    Photos you pick are kept in this browser on this device, as smaller copies (up to 60, newest first), so you don't have to pick them again. Keeping them sends them nowhere: a photo leaves this device only for a step that asks you first.

    Delete my face data

    Removes consent, this tab's session, skin and body history, Skin Moment check-ins and My photos on this device, and asks our server to delete any photo you uploaded in this tab. On-device mode stays as you set it.
